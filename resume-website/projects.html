My Elite Projects

A collection of my digital creations, where code meets creativity.

Omniverse EKYC (2025)

Imagine walking into a Vodacom store, flashing your ID, and being verified faster than you can say microservices. That's the magic of Omniverse EKYC. I built the frontend brains behind this innovative solution, ensuring a seamless user experience. This platform allows customers to verify their identity in real-time, making the onboarding process faster and more efficient. It's a React monorepo that integrates with various microservices, containerized using Docker, and orchestrated with AWS EKS.

I’m proud to have contributed to every stage of its development—from ideation to deployment. Watching the idea blossom into a tool that stores use to speed up their verification process has been incredibly rewarding.

Vodapay Buy Journey (2025)

The Buy Bundles & Airtime journey needed a glow up. So I migrated the whole frontend to TypeScript (fewer bugs, more sanity!). I also implemented a new product called PrepaidLTE (15% adoption, thank you much). And I didn't stop there—I delivered seasonal promotions, turning campaigns into engagement magnets.

Sirvify (2024)

Sirvify is a B2B software solution close to my heart! This platform empowers companies to create dynamic campaigns using Email, SMS, WhatsApp, or a blend of the three. Think Mailchimp, but spicier! Built subscription billing + JWT authentication + live chat. Result: 40% client adoption growth and 30% fewer support tickets.

Xact (2024)

Xact was my first professional project, and what an adventure it was! Built in collaboration with a fellow fresh grad under the insightful guidance of my manager, this React.js admin dashboard allowed our admins to sign up, authenticate, view client data, and perform CRUD operations effortlessly. One of my proudest contributions was implementing a live chat feature, enabling real-time communication with clients via WhatsApp!

Though the dashboard has since evolved into a financial products offering page for our company, the experience taught me valuable lessons in teamwork and real-world application development.

GoonMart (2023)

Need a shopping spree without leaving your chair? My talented friends and I built GoonMart for our final year Software Development course. This React.js online store offers users a seamless shopping experience where they can sign up, browse a diverse range of items, and easily search and filter products to find exactly what they need. With the ability to add items to their carts, shoppers can enjoy a taste of e-commerce magic!

While the checkout process is a mock-up, we focused on making the entire experience as realistic and engaging as possible. So, whether you’re on the hunt for the latest gadgets or just browsing for fun, GoonMart has something for everyone.

Dynamic Programming Tool (2023)

This React.js platform lets users sign up to explore the world of dynamic programming. Whether you're learning the basics or sharpening your skills, this tool offers a fun way to learn and practice core concepts. Ready to tackle those tricky algorithms? Sign up and start solving!

Shark Attack (2023)

For our final year Computer Graphics and Visualisation course, my friends and I dove deep into the ocean to build this Three.js fun-filled game. The player is a shark on a feeding frenzy, gobbling up fish to boost their points—think Maneater with a twist. But watch out for the landmines! Hit one, and your score takes a dive.

Check out the instructions to run the game locally and unleash your inner predator!
